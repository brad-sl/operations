## Important Risk Information

**Please read this document carefully before subscribing to or using ARCH Automation.**

ARCH Automation provides software that can automatically place trades on your Coinbase Advanced account after you authorize access via OAuth. This document describes the principal risks associated with using the Service.

---

## 1. Financial Risk

### 1.1 Risk of Loss

**Cryptocurrency trading involves substantial risk of loss.** You may lose some or all of the capital you allocate to trading. There is no guarantee that any trading strategy, whether automated or manual, will be profitable.

| Risk Factor | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| **Market volatility** | Cryptocurrency prices can experience extreme volatility, including rapid and significant declines, within short time periods |
| **Liquidity risk** | Some trading pairs may have limited liquidity, potentially affecting fill prices and execution |
| **Slippage** | The actual execution price may differ from the expected price, particularly during volatile markets or for larger orders |
| **Gap risk** | Prices may "gap" (move significantly between trades) due to market events, news, or low liquidity periods |

### 1.2 Past Performance

**Past performance is not indicative of future results.** Historical backtests, simulated performance, or prior trading results do not guarantee future trading outcomes. Any performance data presented is for informational purposes only and should not be the sole basis for investment decisions.

### 1.3 No Guarantee of Profit

**ARCH Automation does not guarantee profits.** The Service is a software tool that executes the parameters you configure. Market conditions, strategy performance, and individual results vary. You should not subscribe to the Service with the expectation of guaranteed returns.

## 2. Technical Risk

### 2.1 Service Availability

The Service depends on the continuous availability of:
- **Coinbase Advanced API** — Coinbase's systems may experience outages, maintenance, or rate limiting
- **Internet connectivity** — Your internet connection and ours
- **Hosting infrastructure** — Our servers, which may experience downtime

### 2.2 Execution Delays

Automated trading may be subject to:
- API latency and delays
- Order processing queues
- Network congestion
- Coinbase order validation and routing

### 2.3 Software Limitations

The Service executes trades based on the parameters you configure. The software:
- Cannot predict or prevent market downturns
- May not respond to rapidly changing market conditions
- May have bugs, errors, or limitations not yet identified
- May not be suitable for all market conditions or trading styles

### 2.4 OAuth Token Issues

The Service relies on OAuth tokens that may:
- Expire unexpectedly
- Be revoked by you via Coinbase
- Be affected by Coinbase policy changes
- Fail to refresh automatically in rare cases

## 3. Operational Risk

### 3.1 User Configuration

**You are solely responsible for:**
- Configuring your trading parameters (pairs, caps, risk levels)
- Reviewing your account activity regularly
- Understanding how the trading rules you set will behave
- Ensuring your Coinbase account has sufficient funds
- Complying with your own tax obligations

### 3.2 Stop-Loss and Risk Management

While the Service includes stop-loss and risk management features:
- Stop-loss orders may not execute at the specified price due to market conditions (slippage)
- Rapid market movements may cause orders to fill at less favorable prices
- No risk management system can prevent all losses or protect against all market conditions

### 3.3 Billing and Disconnection

If your subscription payment fails:
- Automated trading will be paused
- Pending orders may be affected
- Some features may become unavailable until payment is resolved
- You should review your Coinbase account after any disconnection
