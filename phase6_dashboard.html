1D %
Whole-account % change vs ~24h ago. Deposit-adjusted (external cash in/out removed). Includes unrealized moves on open positions—not closed-trade PnL alone. Deposit-adjusted wallet return; 3% SL is per-position from entry, not a portfolio DD cap.
--
7D %
Rolling 7-day portfolio return, deposit-adjusted. Early noise window — can look green while the longer path is still repairing. Not the main stabilize gate.
--
14D %
Rolling 14-day deposit-adjusted wallet return. Primary trend-repair gate: claim “stabilizing” only after ~14d of better path — not after one green week. Slow bleed here is common before regime transitions; 7D alone can fool you into quitting right before a turn.
--
30D %
Rolling 30-day portfolio return, deposit-adjusted. Longer context / legacy drawdown. Requires enough snapshot history; shows N/A if the window cannot be computed.
--
Exit WR
Share of recent realizing exits (non-zero PnL in last 100 ledger rows) that won. Count-based—many small wins can read high even when portfolio % is negative. Not the same as 7D/14D %.

Regime
Active market regime (BTC window), boundary layer (soft_up/climb/pre_bull), and live cash stance. PARK = no new buys live. DEPLOY = buys allowed under entry gates (RSI/sentiment/cooldowns) — not a fixed dollar spend ceiling per cycle. We do not show a “cap $N” here: soft budgets exist internally but recovery/rotation paths can exceed them, so a hard $ promise would be false.
--
Preserve
Park policy: mostly USDC/USD cash; optional PAXG gold sleeve. OFF = sleeve off. STANDBY = enabled, not armed. MICRO = small live sleeve for logs (~$75). ARMED = full Hold target. E1 is a deep Coinbase stop (~−32% from arm), not a 3% crypto SL — gold can still drop hard. See docs/research/PARK_REGIME_POLICY.md.

Today vs our plan
Left side: is the market busy today? Right side: are we buying or holding cash? A busy day does not force us to go all-in. "Open slots" means how many coins currently pass our buy checklist.

Why so much cash?
Plain reasons you might see cash instead of full crypto: holding cash on purpose, nothing good enough to buy, hot coins not on your short list, short pause after a sell, or money you set aside. Built for set-and-forget traders — no jargon required.
