## Plain English (go/no-go style)

PAXG moves **moderately** with the gold price proxy we used (daily return correlation **0.785**, beta **0.793**). Daily lag test peaks at **same calendar day** (corr 0.785). That is still effectively **near real-time on a day timescale**, not a multi-week lag.

Over the sample, the average PAXG−gold level gap was about **0.22%** of gold (stdev **0.81%**). Share of days within 0.5% / 1% / 2%: 57% / 83% / 97%. Latest gap: **-0.09%** (PAXG vs proxy).

For Smart Park, treat PAXG as **gold exposure on the exchange**, not magic crypto alpha. It should feel like gold day-to-day. Short gaps open when: gold market is closed and crypto is open, PAXG is thin on Coinbase, or people rush into on-chain gold in a crypto panic (premium) or dump it (discount). Arbitrage (mint/redeem via Paxos when available) usually pulls it back — that process is hours to days for institutions, not weeks, under normal conditions.

## Method caveats

- True LBMA loco London spot is not free here; GC=F is the standard liquid USD gold proxy. It is extremely tightly linked to spot but is futures, not vault gold.
- Daily bars hide **intraday** lag (minutes–hours) and **weekend** PAXG-only moves.
- Coinbase PAXG-USD spread/liquidity can widen vs global PAXG or Paxos mint/redeem fair value.
- Design intent (Paxos): 1 PAXG ≈ 1 fine troy oz allocated gold; arb keeps it near spot under normal access.

## Design intent (context, not our calc)

PAXG is built so one token relates to one fine troy ounce of vaulted gold; public explainers describe **tight arbitrage vs XAU** and same-direction moves with spot, with temporary premiums/discounts in crypto stress or when gold markets are closed.

### How to read this

- **Levels ~1.00** means the *price chart* of PAXG and gold futures sit on top of each other over years — your intuition is right.
- **Daily returns ~0.78–0.85** is still a strong co-move, but not a perfect clone hour-to-hour: futures close, crypto weekends, and Coinbase spreads add noise.
- **Lag:** peak at **0 days** (and typically 0–1 hour on hourly data). There is **no multi-day delay** where PAXG “eventually” catches gold. Gaps are **same-session noise / premium**, not a slow tracker.
