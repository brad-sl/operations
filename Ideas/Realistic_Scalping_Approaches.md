## Honest Assessment

Consistent, reliable scalping is much harder than most people think. The majority of retail scalpers lose money over time due to fees, slippage, emotional decisions, and lack of real edge.

Successful traders in this space usually have:
- Very strict rules
- Excellent risk management
- Either low fees or high volume

## Ranked Methods by Realism

### 1. Short-term Momentum / Breakout Scalping (Most realistic starting point)
Focus on **5–15 minute** moves rather than true 30-second scalps.

- Trade during high-liquidity sessions (London + NY overlap)
- Use clear structure (previous high/low, VWAP, volume spikes)
- Only take trades with strong momentum confirmation
- Fixed risk per trade (e.g., 0.5–1% of account)

This is more controllable than pure scalping.

### 2. Mean Reversion on Liquid Pairs
Trade back to the mean on high-volume pairs (BTC, ETH, SOL) during ranging periods.

- Use RSI + volume
- Clear invalidation levels
- Works better than chasing breakouts in choppy markets

Easier to systematize than pure momentum.

### 3. Volume Profile + Order Flow Style
Focus on high-volume nodes and imbalances rather than trying to predict direction constantly.

- Requires better tools (but still self-controlled)
- More structural than pure price action

### 4. Fully Systematic / Quant Approach (Highest control, hardest to build)
Build or use rules-based systems with:
- Clear entry/exit rules
- Backtested edge
- Strict position sizing

This is the most "reliable" in theory, but very few retail traders actually maintain an edge after costs.

## Key Principle

**Risk management > Strategy**

Even a mediocre strategy with excellent risk rules will outperform a "great" strategy with poor risk management.
