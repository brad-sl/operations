## General
**What is ARCH Automation?**  
Software that can run rules-based portfolio automation on your Coinbase Advanced account after you connect with OAuth.

**Do you hold my crypto?**  
No. Funds stay in your Coinbase account. We are non-custodial.

**Is this investment advice?**  
No. ARCH is software access, not an adviser, broker, or fund.

**Do you guarantee profits?**  
No. Cryptocurrency trading involves substantial risk of loss.

## Connect & security
**Why OAuth instead of API keys?**  
OAuth lets you authorize and revoke access in Coinbase without pasting keys into our CRM or chat.

**What permissions do you need?**  
View + trade related scopes required to automate. We do not request transfer/withdrawal ability. Exact scope labels follow Coinbase’s current OAuth app configuration.

**Can I revoke access?**  
Yes, anytime in Coinbase connected apps. You should also cancel the subscription if you no longer want the service.

## Billing & plans
**What do I pay for?**  
Software and service access (subscription). Exchange trading fees on Coinbase are separate and charged by Coinbase.

**What if my card fails?**  
Automated trading pauses until payment is resolved. See dunning emails and Terms.

**Refunds?**  
See Terms. Pilot draft: possible review if you never completed OAuth within the stated window.

## Risk & expectations
**Will stop-losses save me from all losses?**  
No. Stops can slip; markets gap; no system prevents all losses.

**Can I use this without Coinbase Advanced?**  
Product is built for Coinbase Advanced. You need an eligible Coinbase account.

**Are you affiliated with Coinbase?**  
No.
