# CLAIMS & SCREENSHOT POLICY — ARCH Automation

**Version:** 1.0 (Brad Approved)
**Date:** 2026-07-16
**Policy Type:** Zero-tolerance. No performance guarantees, no personal P&L in marketing.

---

## 1. Policy Statement

ARCH Automation marketing uses **deposit-adjusted honesty** and **process metrics only**. We never fabricate performance, use personal trading screenshots as product proof, or guarantee returns. This policy applies to all marketing assets: landing pages, ads, email/SMS, social media, affiliate materials, and partner communications.

**Zero tolerance.** Any violation of this policy must be reported and corrected immediately. Repeat violations will result in content removal, escalation, and process review.

---

## 2. Approved Language

The following claims are **allowed** with product truth:

| Category | Approved Claim | Requirement |
|----------|---------------|-------------|
| **Connection** | "Connect Coinbase Advanced via OAuth; capital stays in your Coinbase account." | Must be accurate |
| **Automation** | "Automated rebalancing and risk rules subject to your tier limits and market conditions." | Must reference configurable parameters |
| **Billing** | "Pause trading if billing lapses or connection errors; you can revoke access at Coinbase." | Must be accurate |
| **Process** | Onboarding time targets, uptime/health %, support response SLAs (once defined). | Must be measured and verifiable |
| **Education** | How rebalancing / SL mechanics work (neutral, no projected ROI). | Educational only, no performance claims |
| **Software** | "Subscribe, connect Coinbase with OAuth, let disciplined rebalancing and risk rules run on *your* account." | Must clarify software subscription |
| **Non-custodial** | "We never custody your funds. Funds stay at Coinbase." | Must be clear |

---

## 3. Red Flags — Forbidden Language

**Never use** in any marketing asset:

| Red Flag | Example | Why |
|----------|---------|-----|
| **Guaranteed returns** | "X% guaranteed," "risk-free," "no downside" | Misleading, illegal in many jurisdictions |
| **Fixed ROI** | "Earn 5% monthly," "AI prints money" | False guarantee |
| **Personal P&L screenshots** | Brad's Phase 6 account performance | Misleading — personal account ≠ product performance |
| **Backtest numbers** | "Our strategy returned X% over Y years" | Cherry-picked, not representative |
| **"Average user"** | "Our users earn X%" | Cannot be substantiated without statistically valid data |
| **"AI never loses"** | Any claim of invincibility | False |
| **Custody claims** | "Deposit with us," "We hold your funds" | We are non-custodial |
| **Advisory claims** | "Let us manage your portfolio," "We advise" | Software, not advisory |
| **Fund language** | "Pool," "fund," "managed account," "performance fee" | Not a fund |
| **Exchange confusion** | "Built-in exchange," "trading platform" | We are software on Coinbase |
| **API key solicitation** | "Enter your API key" | OAuth only — no API keys in CRM |

## 4. Screenshot Policy

### 4.1 General Rule
**No screenshots from Brad's personal Phase 6 trading account may be used in marketing materials.** This includes:
- Live P&L screenshots
- Portfolio balance screenshots
- Trade history screenshots
- Dashboard screenshots showing personal account data

### 4.2 Allowed Screenshots (with approval)
The following types of screenshots may be used **only with written approval from Brad**:

| Type | Conditions |
|------|------------|
| **Process/UX screenshots** | Showing the OAuth connect flow, status dashboard (without balance data), or setting configuration. Must not show personal P&L or balances. |
| **Generic UI mockups** | Using placeholder/test data, not real account data. Must be clearly marked as "Sample interface — not actual trading data." |
| **Pilot trader case studies** | Only with **explicit written consent** from the pilot trader. Must use approved metrics only (no cherry-picked performance). Must include disclaimer: "Past performance is not indicative of future results." |

### 4.3 Deposit-Adjusted Honesty

When displaying any status or health metrics:
- **Deposit-adjusted:** If a user deposits $1k and the portfolio shows $1.05k, the return is +5%, not "$1.05k" as a raw number implying total value.
- **N/A treatment:** When data is insufficient to calculate a metric, display "N/A" or "Insufficient data" — never "0%" or "0.00" as if the metric is zero.
- **Context:** Always include clear context about what is being displayed, the time period, and any limitations.

## 5. Always-On Disclaimer (Required on All Assets)

Every landing page, checkout page, ad, email, and social post must include:

> **Disclaimer:** ARCH Automation provides software that can place trades on your Coinbase Advanced account after you authorize access. Cryptocurrency trading involves substantial risk of loss. Past performance is not indicative of future results. We do not provide investment advice, do not custody your assets, and do not guarantee profits. Subscription fees are for software and service access only.

**Placement:**
- **Landing pages:** Footer, visible without scrolling
- **Checkout:** Immediately above the "Submit Payment" / "Confirm" button
- **Emails:** Footer (standard for financial communications)
- **Ads:** As required by platform policy (at minimum, "Trading involves risk. See disclosures.")

## 8. Policy Violations

| Severity | Examples | Action |
|----------|----------|--------|
| **Critical** | Guaranteed returns, personal P&L in ad, custody claim | Immediate removal, escalation to Brad, process review |
| **High** | Unsubstantiated claim, missing disclaimer on checkout | Correction within 24h, compliance note filed |
| **Medium** | Missing disclaimer on email, outdated tier reference | Correction within 72h |
| **Low** | Minor wording ambiguity, typo in disclaimer | Correction at next revision |

**Zero tolerance means:** Any critical violation requires immediate halt of the affected asset and a review of the entire content pipeline before resuming.
